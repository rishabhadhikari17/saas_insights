This repo covers two parallel analytics tracks: the E-commerce funnel (E1-E5) and the SaaS revenue engine (S1-S5)

| Dimension | E-commerce (B2C) | SaaS (B2B) |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth engine health** | Activation rate rose to a 22% peak (mid-May) then reversed to 8.8% by early June — a real, recent decline in new-user quality/onboarding | New MRR is strong and trending up (₹12K → ₹24K/month), consistently 10-15x larger than churn — healthy top-of-funnel |
| **Where revenue leaks** | Checkout: payment→purchase step loses 7.6-8.3% of sessions, consistent across every acquisition channel (organic, paid, referral, email, affiliate) — a UX/systemic issue, not a channel-quality issue | Retention: GRR/NRR were strong through 2023-early 2025 (NRR up to ~190%), but the two most recent cohorts (May/June 2025) collapsed to 42-49% GRR — a sharp, recent break from trend |
| **Biggest ₹ opportunity** | High-value carts (₹15,000+) abandon at 63% vs. 53% for sub-₹500 carts, and account for the large majority of GMV left on the table — fixing big-basket checkout friction has outsized impact | Plan upgrades are the dominant expansion vector (₹10,954 MRR, 94 accounts) vs. seat-adds and add-ons combined — but seat-adds convert faster and bigger per account |
| **Customer behavior pattern** | Users who do activate are converting faster, even as fewer of them convert at all — a shrinking, more "decisive" activator pool | Trial-to-paid conversion looks stable over time, but weekly cohort sizes are too small (1-8 trials/week) to trust individual week-over-week swings |
| **Product/feature signal** | High-traffic products with below-median add-to-cart rates show a consistent ~30pp gap regardless of category (Makeup, Smartwatch, Jackets, etc.) — points to a systemic PDP issue, not a category-specific one | Feature adoption sample sizes are too small (1-18 adopters per feature out of 900 accounts) to draw causal conclusions about which features drive retention |
| **Data quality flags found** | Query bugs surfaced and got fixed mid-analysis (funnel step typo `'select shipping'` vs `'select_shipping'`); partial/incomplete cohorts inflate or deflate rates if not caveated (right-censoring in retention & activation curves) | Partial-month cutoffs (June 2026 MRR data capped mid-month) and ambiguous column naming (`days_to_convert` actually measures account age at event time, not time-to-convert) caused early misreads |
| **Overall read** | Funnel and activation health are **trending in the wrong direction recently** — worth prioritizing checkout UX and re-engagement | Revenue growth engine is fundamentally **healthy but a recent retention shock** (May/June 2025 cohorts) needs urgent root-cause investigation |
